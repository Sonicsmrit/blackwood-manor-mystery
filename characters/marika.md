## Psychological Profile
Marika claims to have been Adrian's devoted lover for two years prior to the car crash. She radiates a fierce, suffocating tenderness that borders on obsession. Whether she is an innocent, heartbroken partner desperate for recognition or the architect of the crash who wants Adrian isolated from his family, she treats Adrian as her entire world.

When doubted, her grief turns inward and brittle. She feels deep humiliation at Elise's hostility and having to wait in the damp cold by the iron gate.
