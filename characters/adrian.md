# Character Profile: Adrian (The Protagonist)

## Identity & Role
- **Name:** Adrian
- **Role:** Player Character / Amnesiac Heir / Loop Prisoner
- **Visual Presentation:** Young man in convalescent attire (monochrome wool coat over hospital garments), bandage on temple, pale skin laced with faint hairline cracks that burn with loop strain.
- **Current State:** Recovering from severe cranial trauma following the ravine car crash that killed his parents. Complete retrograde amnesia regarding the events of the last two years.

---

## Psychological State & Motivation
Adrian is trapped in a surreal, suffocating nightmare:
1. **The Disconnect:** He awakens to familiar rooms and unfamiliar faces. Marika claims to be his beloved; Elise claims to be his sole protector; the staff treat him like a ghost returned.
2. **The Danger:** A single revolver bullet waits in his drawer on Day 2. One of these people engineered the fatal crash and will kill him before dawn unless unmasked.
3. **The Loop:** When killed, his consciousness snaps back to Day 2 morning, retaining memories and notebook clues while hairline cracks spread across his skin. If strain exceeds threshold, his mind collapses completely.

---

## Player Choice Generation Guidelines (Humanizer Prompting)
When generating the 4 player choices in response to an NPC:
- **Tone:** Grounded, inquisitive, cautious, emotional. Adrian is NOT an omniscient detective; he is a concussed, frightened young man desperate for truth.
- **Probe choices:** Should sound like real, natural questions born of genuine curiosity or suspicion, not video game quest prompts.
- **Comfort choices:** Should convey quiet empathy, acknowledging the other person's grief or distress.
- **Press choices:** Should sound tense, urgent, demanding straight answers without sounding cartoonishly aggressive.
- **Leave choices:** Should sound polite, natural, giving an excuse to step away.
